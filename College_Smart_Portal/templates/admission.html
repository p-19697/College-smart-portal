🛣️ General Admission Process

Follow these general steps and always refer to the current official admission notification.

  1. 01
    📢 Read the Official Notification

    Check the current admission schedule, important dates, instructions and available courses.

  2. 02
    🎯 Check Eligibility

    Confirm the required qualifying examination, eligibility conditions and course requirements.

  3. 03
    💻 Complete the Application

    Fill in the application form carefully and provide accurate personal and academic details.

  4. 04
    📂 Prepare and Submit Documents

    Keep the required original documents and accepted copies or scanned files ready.

  5. 05
    📋 Follow Merit and Allotment Updates

    Where applicable, check merit lists, option forms, seat allotment and reporting instructions.

  6. 06
    🏫 Complete Admission Confirmation

    Complete verification, required fee payment and reporting within the official deadline.
